The Noctalia launcher is a centralized search interface that provides quick access to your applications, system actions, and utility providers.

## How search works

As you type, Noctalia queries all active **Launcher Providers**. Providers can either be "global" (always searching) or "prefixed" (triggered by a specific keyword).

- **Global providers** (like Applications and Calculator) results are merged and sorted by a relevance score.
- **Prefixed providers** (like Emoji and Wallpaper) take over the search results when their prefix is detected at the start of your query.

Type `/` by itself to show a lightweight overview of available prefixed providers. Activating one inserts its prefix into the launcher input.

### Sections and the action bar

Like Raycast, a global search lists everything it found under **Results**, ranked by relevance, with matching files after them under **Files**. With nothing typed, pinned apps appear under **Favourites**, the last few things you ran (apps, system commands, quicklinks, snippets, scripts, panels) follow under **Suggestions**, and the other applications come after. Prefixed searches keep a single list.

The bar along the bottom names the selected result's kind and its keys: **Return** runs the primary action (its name, such as **Open Application** or **Paste Snippet**, is shown there) and **Shift + Return** opens the actions menu. When a search has a query, a **Use "…" with…** section at the end offers the fallback commands: **Search the Web**, **Search Files for "…"**, and any search quicklinks listed in `shell.launcher.fallbacks` (for example `["files", "github"]`).

### Navigation

- **Up** / **Down**, or **Ctrl + P** / **Ctrl + N**, move the selection; **Page Up** / **Page Down** move by a page.
- **Tab** autocompletes the selected result: a provider row from the `/` overview puts its prefix in the field, a search quicklink puts its keyword in (`gh `), and a fallback row carries your query into that provider.
- **Esc** inside a provider view you reached from the root search (for example after typing `/clip`) goes back to the root search; **Esc** at the root, or in a view the launcher opened with (the clipboard shortcut), closes the launcher.

### Arguments

Commands that take arguments (a script command with `@raycast.argument1`, a search quicklink picked without a query, **Translate** in AI) show them inline, like Raycast: **Tab** on the command, or **Return** while a required argument is still empty, opens an argument bar under the search field with the command on the left and a pill per argument. The search field edits the highlighted argument; **Tab** / **Shift + Tab** (or **Down** / **Up**) move between them, **Return** runs the command once every required argument has a value, and **Esc** puts your search back. Typing the arguments after the title (`deploy staging`, `gh noctalia`) still works.

### Action panel

**Shift + Return** (or a right click) opens the action panel over the bottom-right of the results, like Raycast's ⌘K panel: the primary action first with its **↵** hint, then the result's own actions (the first of which **Ctrl + Return** runs directly; for example **Quit Application** and **Copy Name** for a running application, **Show in Folder**, **Copy Path** and **Move to Trash** for files, **Pin** and **Save as Snippet** for clipboard entries, **Close Window** for windows), then **Pin to Launcher**, **Set Alias…** and **Copy Hotkey Command**. While the panel is open the search field filters the actions; **Up** / **Down** move, **Return** runs, **Esc** or **Shift + Return** closes it and brings the search back.

### Aliases and hotkeys

**Set Alias…** gives a result a short word, shown as a small tag beside it. Typing the alias exactly puts that result at the top, above everything else. Aliases set this way are kept in `~/.local/state/noctalia/launcher_aliases.json`; aliases in `[shell.launcher.aliases]` take precedence. Applications, quicklinks, panels and session actions can have aliases.

**Copy Hotkey Command** copies a `noctalia msg launcher-run <alias or Provider:id>` command. Bind it in your compositor to run that result from anywhere, without opening the launcher:

```ini
# hyprland.conf
bind = SUPER, N, exec, noctalia msg launcher-run notes
bind = SUPER SHIFT, D, exec, noctalia msg launcher-run System:toggle-dark-mode
```

### Forms

**Create Quicklink**, **Create Snippet** and their **Edit** actions show a short form in place of the results, Raycast-style. The search field edits the highlighted field: **Return** or **Tab** moves to the next field, **Shift + Tab** or **Up** goes back, and **Return** on the last row saves. **Esc** cancels. A problem, such as a keyword that's already taken, shows beside the save row.

### Preview pane

Scoped searches for clipboard history, snippets and files show a preview pane beside the list: the full text or image, the first lines of a text file, and details such as size, type and when it was modified.

### Usage-based boosting

The launcher tracks how often you activate specific results (applications and wallpapers). Frequently used items receive a score boost, surfacing them at the top of the results list even with partial queries.

### Category filters

Category filters are off by default, so the launcher is just the search field and the list, like Raycast. Turn them on with `shell.launcher.categories` (**Settings → Launcher**), or press **F6** while the launcher is open, to show compact filter buttons for providers that expose categories.

- **Applications** can be filtered by desktop-entry categories such as Internet, Development, Games, Office, System, and Utilities.
- **Emoji** can be filtered by emoji categories such as people, animals, food, travel, objects, symbols, and flags.
- **All** leaves the active result set unfiltered.

Press `F6` while the launcher is open to reveal hidden filters without changing the active filter. Once the filters are visible, use `F6` to move to the next filter and `Shift+F6` to move to the previous filter.

:::note[Application options]

Right-click an application result to open its menu of additional per-app options. The same menu opens with **Shift + Validate** (default: `Shift+Return`).

:::

## Providers

### Applications

The primary provider for finding and launching your installed apps.

When `shell.launcher.app_grid` is enabled and every visible result is an application, results switch from the default list to a multi-column icon grid with the app name underneath. Mixed searches (calculator hits, emoji, session actions, prefixed providers, and so on) keep the list layout. Left/right keybinds move between columns in grid mode; up/down move by row.

- **Source**: Reads `.desktop` entries from standard XDG directories (e.g., `/usr/share/applications`, `~/.local/share/applications`).
- **Package origins**: Results identify Flatpak, Snap, Nix, and AppImage applications when their desktop entries or executable metadata provide that information. AppImages are recognized from their AppImage desktop-entry metadata or executable signature, including extensionless files managed by AppImageLauncher.
- **Search**: Matches against the application name, generic name, keywords, and categories.
- **Desktop actions**: Set `shell.launcher.show_app_actions = true` to search and launch `.desktop` actions directly alongside applications. This is disabled by default; actions remain available from an application's right-click menu.
- **Scoring**: Uses fuzzy matching with weights. Activation count boosts the score.
- **Pinned apps**: Drag a pinned app onto another pinned app to reorder them. Pin markers appear in both the list and app-grid views.
- **Terminal apps**: Entries with `Terminal=true` run inside a terminal. Prefer `export TERMINAL=wezterm` (or another emulator on `PATH`); do not put `-e` in `$TERMINAL`. See [Shell: launching apps](/noctalia/configuration/shell/#shell-settings) for discovery order and how this differs from `launch_apps_custom_command`.

### Files

Find files and folders in your home folder by name.

- **Prefix**: `/file ` (also included in global search; turn it off with `[shell.launcher.providers.files] global = false`)
- **Source**: Indexes your home folder on a background thread the first time you search, and again when the index is more than five minutes old. Hidden files and folders, dependency and build trees (`node_modules`, `target`, `build`, …) and anything more than six folders deep are skipped.
- **Search**: Matches the query as part of a file or folder name; names that start with it rank first. Global search shows the best six, `/file` up to fifty.
- **Functionality**: Activating a result opens it with `xdg-open`. The actions menu adds **Show in Folder**, **Open in Terminal** (folders), **Copy Path**, **Copy File** and **Move to Trash**. `/file` shows a preview pane with image and text previews.

### Calculator

A built-in expression evaluator powered by [`libqalculate`](https://qalculate.github.io/).

- **Prefix**: `/calc ` (also available globally).
- **Trigger**: Automatically activates in global search when the query contains a digit. The explicit `/calc` prefix
  also accepts digit-free expressions such as `/calc EUR`.
- **Functionality**: Standard arithmetic and functions (`sin`, `cos`, `sqrt`, `pi`, `e`, …), plus
  unit conversion (`10 cm to in`, `5 ft in cm`, `100 km/h to mph`, `70F in C`), number bases (`0xff`), and currency conversion
  (`10 USD to EUR`, `20 GBP in JPY`). Both `to` and `in` work. Decimal and thousands separators follow the system numeric locale
  (for example `10000,50+2000,25` under `pt_BR`). Pressing **Enter** copies the result to your clipboard.
- **History**: `/calc` with nothing typed lists the last answers you copied, newest first, each with **Copy Answer** and **Copy Expression**; **Clear History** is at the end. The list is kept in `~/.local/state/noctalia/calculator_history.json`.
- **Currency rates**: Exchange rates are cached locally and refreshed in the background from libqalculate's
  ECB/global providers when `shell.launcher.fetch_exchange_rates` is enabled (the default). Refresh is also
  skipped when `[shell].offline_mode` is enabled; in either case the last cached rates are used.

### Emoji

A quick way to find and copy emojis.

- **Prefix**: `/emo `
- **Functionality**: Searches `emoji.json` by name, category, and keywords. Activating a result copies the emoji character directly to your clipboard.

Example: `/emo heart`

### Panels

Open or close any panel Noctalia knows about, without memorizing its `noctalia msg panel-toggle` id.

- **Prefix**: `/pan `
- **Source**: Every panel currently registered with Noctalia (built-in or from an enabled plugin), plus one row per visible Control Center tab (Audio, Network, Bluetooth, and so on).
- **Functionality**: Fuzzy-matches against the panel's name. Activating a plain panel result toggles it; activating a tab result opens Control Center directly to that tab.
- **Scoring**: Activation count boosts the score.
- **Ignored panels**: `shell.launcher.panels.ignored` excludes specific panel ids from the list (defaulting to `polkit`, `setup-wizard`, `test`, and `launcher`) for panels that aren't meant to be opened on demand, or (for `launcher`) redundant to toggle from within the launcher itself. Editable under **Settings → Panels → Launcher → Providers**.

### Wallpaper

Quickly browse and apply wallpapers from your configured directories.

- **Prefix**: `/wall `
- **Source**: Searches the directories defined in your `[wallpaper]` config.
- **Functionality**: Filters by filename and parent directory name. Activating a result immediately applies the wallpaper to all monitors and updates your theme if using `wallpaper` as the theme source.
- **Scoring**: Activation count boosts the score.

### Session

Run enabled session menu actions from the launcher.

- **Prefix**: `/session `
- **Source**: Uses the configured `shell.session.actions` entries, including custom labels, icons, and commands.
- **Functionality**: Activating a result runs the same action path as the session panel, including logout, suspend, reboot, shutdown, lock, custom command entries, and configured hooks.

### Windows

Find and focus any currently open window.

- **Prefix**: `/win `
- **Source**: Lists the windows reported by your compositor across every monitor and workspace.
- **Functionality**: Fuzzy-matches against the window title and app id. Activating a result focuses the window, switching to its workspace if needed. The actions menu adds **Close Window**.

### Clipboard history

Search everything you've copied, with a preview of the selected entry.

- **Prefix**: `/clip ` (not in global search)
- **Source**: The shell's clipboard history (`shell.clipboard_enabled`), newest first, pinned entries kept.
- **Functionality**: Return pastes the entry, following `shell.clipboard_auto_paste` (or copies it when that is `off`). Actions: **Copy to Clipboard**, **Open in Browser** for links, **Edit Image** for images when `shell.clipboard_image_action_command` is set, **Pin**/**Unpin**, **Save as Snippet**, **Delete Entry** and **Clear History** (pinned entries stay).
- **Type filter**: Press **F6** to show the **Text**, **Link** and **Image** filters, like Raycast's type menu, and **F6** again to move between them.
- **Replaces the Clipboard panel**: the clipboard bar widget, the Control Center shortcut and `noctalia msg panel-toggle clipboard` open the launcher here.

### Quicklinks and web search

Open a URL, or search a site, from a keyword.

- **Prefix**: `/link ` (also in global search)
- **Built in**: `g` Google, `ddg` DuckDuckGo, `gh` GitHub, `yt` YouTube, `wiki` Wikipedia, `aw` ArchWiki and `maps` OpenStreetMap.
- **Functionality**: Type a keyword, a space and your query (`gh noctalia`) to search that site. Activating a search quicklink with no query fills in its keyword so you can type one.
- **Creating**: Run **Create Quicklink** (first in `/link`, or search "create quicklink") to fill in a name, a link and an optional keyword. A link containing `{query}` becomes a search. **Edit Quicklink…** and **Delete Quicklink** in the actions menu change ones made here; editing a built-in saves your version in its place. They are kept in `~/.local/state/noctalia/launcher_quicklinks.json`. Quicklinks can also go under `[shell.launcher.quicklinks.<id>]` in your config, and those are edited there.
- **Web search**: Any query of two or more characters also offers **Search the Web** at the end of the results, using `shell.launcher.web_search_url`.

### Snippets

Paste saved text by name or keyword.

- **Prefix**: `/snip ` (also in global search)
- **Source**: `[shell.launcher.snippets.<id>]` in your config, plus snippets made in the launcher (kept in `~/.local/state/noctalia/launcher_snippets.json`).
- **Creating**: Run **Create Snippet** (first in `/snip`, or search "create snippet") to fill in a name, the text and an optional keyword. Type `\n` for a new line. **Save as Snippet** in clipboard history opens the same form with the copied text filled in, and **Edit Snippet…** changes one made here.
- **Placeholders**: `{date}`, `{time}`, `{datetime}`, `{day}`, `{weekday}`, `{month}`, `{year}`, `{clipboard}` and `{uuid}` are filled in when the snippet is pasted, as in Raycast. `{cursor}` is accepted and removed; the launcher can't place the caret in another app.
- **Expansion while typing**: With `shell.launcher.snippet_expansion = true` (**Settings → Launcher → Expand Snippets While Typing**), typing a snippet's keyword in any app replaces it with the snippet's text, as in Raycast: the keyword is erased with Backspace through the virtual keyboard and the text pasted. Wayland has no way to watch the keyboard, so, like espanso, the shell reads the keyboards under `/dev/input`; your user must be in the `input` group (`sudo usermod -aG input $USER`, then log in again). Keys decode with the compositor's keymap, a word ends at Return, arrows or any Ctrl/Alt/Super chord, and nothing expands while a shell panel has the keyboard. Keywords should be at least two characters and are matched exactly, so a leading symbol (`;sig`, `:addr`) avoids accidents.

### Script commands

Run your own scripts from the launcher, using Raycast's script command format.

- **Prefix**: `/script ` (also in global search)
- **Source**: Executable files in `~/.config/noctalia/scripts` and any folders in `shell.launcher.script_directories`.
- **Metadata**: Comment lines such as `# @raycast.title`, `@raycast.description`, `@raycast.packageName`, `@raycast.icon` and `@raycast.mode` (`silent`, `compact` or `fullOutput`) are read from the top of the file; `@noctalia.` works too. Raycast script commands work unchanged.
- **Arguments**: `@raycast.argument1` … `argument3` become inline fields in the argument bar (Tab on the command); text typed after the title is passed too (`deploy staging`).
- **Output**: `compact` shows the last line of output as a notification, `fullOutput` shows all of it, and a failing script reports its error. Actions: **Edit Script** and **Show in Folder**.

### Time zones

See the time anywhere, or convert a time between places.

- **Prefix**: `/time ` (also in global search)
- **Examples**: `time tokyo`, `tokyo`, `3pm london in new york`, `9:30 pst to cet`.
- **Functionality**: Knows common cities and zone abbreviations plus every IANA zone name. Return copies the time.

### System

Toggle settings and run system actions.

- **Prefix**: `/sys ` (also in global search)
- **Commands**: Toggle Dark Mode, Do Not Disturb, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Keep Awake, Night Light, Mute and Microphone; Volume Up and Down, Brightness Up and Down; Take Screenshot, Screenshot and Annotate, Record Screen, Pick Color (with `hyprpicker`), Stop Recording, Turn Off Displays, Clear Clipboard History, Clear Notifications, Empty Trash, Eject All Disks and Reload Configuration.
- **Matching**: In global search every word you type must start a word of the command's name or keywords, so short queries don't pull in unrelated commands.

### Timers

Start a countdown that lives in the Dynamic Island.

- **Prefix**: `/timer ` (also in global search when the word "timer" is typed)
- **Examples**: `timer 10m`, `set timer 1h30 tea`, `15 min stretch timer`; after the prefix just `25` or `2h focus`.
- **Functionality**: The timer counts down as a live activity in the Island, a ring that empties, with its label and the time left. A notification says when it is done. `/timer` lists running timers; their actions are **Cancel Timer** and **Add 1 Minute**. Timers live in the shell process and don't survive a restart.

### Kill Process

- **Prefix**: `/kill ` (never in global search)
- **Source**: Your own processes from `/proc`, busiest first, with their PID, average CPU share and resident memory.
- **Functionality**: Search by name, command line or PID. **Return** sends SIGTERM; **Force Kill** in the action panel sends SIGKILL.

### Dates

Date arithmetic in the search field, like Raycast's calculator.

- **Prefix**: `/date ` (also in global search)
- **Examples**: `3 days from now`, `2 weeks ago`, `in 1 month`, `days until 25 dec`, `weeks since 2026-01-01`, `next friday`, `last monday`, `today + 10 days`, `25 dec - 2 weeks`, `1 month from 31 oct`.
- **Functionality**: Shows the resulting date with how far away it is, or the number of days or weeks between two dates. Dates are read day first (`25/12`, `25 dec`, `december 25th 2027`, `2026-12-25`); a day without a year means the next one. Return copies the answer.

### Dictionary

- **Prefix**: `/define ` (also in global search as `define …`, `def …` or `meaning of …`)
- **Source**: The free [dictionaryapi.dev](https://dictionaryapi.dev) service, asked once you pause typing. Nothing is fetched when `[shell].offline_mode` is on.
- **Functionality**: Lists the word's meanings by part of speech, with its pronunciation on the first. Return copies the definition; the action panel offers **Copy Word** and **Copy Example**. While the lookup is in flight, or when the word isn't found or the service is unreachable, a status row takes its place and Return searches the web for the word instead.

### Screenshots

- **Prefix**: `/shot `
- **Source**: Image files in the screenshot folder (`shell.screenshot.directory`, or your Pictures folder), newest first, with a preview pane.
- **Functionality**: Return opens the screenshot. Actions: **Copy Image** (the PNG itself, other formats as a file reference), **Show in Folder**, **Copy Path** and **Move to Trash**.

### Quick notes

The capture half of Raycast's Floating Notes: jot something down without leaving the keyboard.

- **Prefix**: `/note ` (also in global search as `note …`)
- **File**: `shell.launcher.notes_file`, or `~/Documents/Notes.md` (falling back to `~/Notes.md`). Each note is a Markdown list item with a time stamp, so the file reads well anywhere.
- **Functionality**: `note call the dentist` offers **Save note**; `/note` lists the latest notes newest first with a preview, **Copy Note**, **Delete Note** and **Open Notes File**.
- **Floating Notes**: **Floating Notes** (first in `/note`, or search for it, or `noctalia msg panel-open floating-notes`) opens a small note window that stays over your apps while you open other panels or the launcher. It edits the notes file in place and saves as you type; **Esc** or the close button puts it away. `shell.launcher.floating_notes_position` moves it (default `bottom_right`).

### AI

Raycast's AI, answered by the service in `[shell.launcher.ai]`: a local model through [Ollama](https://ollama.com) by default, so nothing leaves your machine, or an online service when you set `provider = "openai"` (OpenAI, and anything speaking its API: OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, LM Studio, …) or `provider = "anthropic"`.

- **Prefix**: `/ai ` (also in global search: **Ask AI** sits with the fallbacks under "Use … with")
- **Setup (local)**: Install Ollama, run `ollama serve` and pull a model (`ollama pull llama3.2`). `/ai › Choose Model` lists the installed models and remembers your pick; `shell.launcher.ai.model` pins one.
- **Setup (online)**: Set `provider` and give it a key: `api_key` in the config, `api_key_command` (a command whose output is the key, such as `pass show openai`, so the key stays out of `config.toml`), or the service's usual environment variable (`OPENAI_API_KEY`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`). `url` defaults to the service's API; point it at `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1`, `https://api.groq.com/openai/v1`, a local LM Studio and so on for other OpenAI-compatible services. `Choose Model` lists the service's models and remembers the pick per service; without one, `gpt-4o-mini` or `claude-sonnet-5-5` answers. Online requests carry only the prompt (your question, or the clipboard text for a command), and `[shell].offline_mode` blocks them like every other request.
- **Quick AI**: Type a question and **Return** on **Ask AI**. The answer streams into the launcher as Markdown; **Return** copies it (and pastes it, per `auto_paste`), the action panel offers **Stop Writing**, **Regenerate** and **Copy Question**. **Esc** goes back to the search; the same question shows its answer again.
- **AI Commands**: **Improve Writing**, **Fix Spelling and Grammar**, **Make Shorter**, **Make Longer**, **Change Tone to Professional / Casual / Friendly / Confident**, **Summarize**, **Explain This in Simple Terms**, **Explain Code Step by Step** and **Translate** (asks for the language in the argument bar) run their prompt over the text on the clipboard, since Wayland doesn't expose the selection of another app: copy the text, run the command, then paste the result over it. The prompts follow the community Ollama extension for Raycast.
- **States**: An unreachable server, a missing or rejected API key, no installed models and offline mode each show a status row saying what to do.

### Window management

Arrange the focused window from the keyboard, with Raycast's Window Management commands.

- **Prefix**: `/wm ` (also in global search)
- **Compositor**: Hyprland only; the provider is empty elsewhere. It talks to Hyprland's own IPC, so it needs no plugin.
- **Layouts**: Left Half, Right Half, Top Half, Bottom Half; the four quarters; First, Center and Last Third; First and Last Two Thirds; Maximize, Almost Maximize, Center, Make Larger and Make Smaller. A layout floats the window first (as every macOS window is "floating") and sizes it inside the monitor's work area, below the Island or bar.
- **Other commands**: Toggle Fullscreen, Toggle Floating, Pin Window (keep on every workspace, above other windows), Next Display and Previous Display.
- **Hotkeys**: Use **Copy Hotkey Command** to bind one, for example `bind = SUPER, Left, exec, noctalia msg launcher-run WindowManagement:left-half`.

## Dmenu mode

`noctalia dmenu` is a stdin/stdout picker for scripts that already produce newline-separated choices:

```sh
printf 'alpha\nbeta\ngamma\n' | noctalia dmenu -p "Pick"
```

It opens the launcher scoped to those candidates, prints the selected line to stdout, and exits `0`. Closing the launcher
or pressing Escape prints nothing and exits `1`. `-p` / `--prompt` sets the launcher input placeholder for that picker.
A tab inside a candidate splits the display into title and description, but the full original line is still returned. If
the current filter has no candidate match, pressing Enter returns the typed text.

The command talks to the running Noctalia instance on the same `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` and `WAYLAND_DISPLAY`; start Noctalia
first, then call `noctalia dmenu` from scripts or compositor commands.

### Persistent dmenu entries

You can also add persistent dmenu-style launcher providers from config. These are useful for custom command palettes,
small script menus, SSH host lists, package-maintenance commands, and other actions you want to run from the launcher
without writing a plugin.

Each entry lives under `[shell.launcher.dmenu.entry.<id>]`, where `<id>` is the provider's stable config id:

```toml
[shell.launcher.dmenu.entry.commands]
command = "printf 'Open Config\nShow Date\n'"
label = "Commands"
prefix = "cmd"
glyph = "terminal"
global = false
exec = "case \"{selection}\" in Open*) xdg-open ~/.config/noctalia ;; Show*) notify-send \"$(date)\" ;; esac"
```

Open the launcher, type `/cmd`, then select one of the listed results. `command` is run to produce newline-separated
candidates. A tab in a candidate splits the display into title and description:

```toml
[shell.launcher.dmenu.entry.commands]
command = "printf 'DNF Upgrade\tRun dnf upgrade --refresh\n'"
label = "Commands"
prefix = "cmd"
glyph = "terminal"
global = false
exec = "foot -e bash -lc 'pkexec dnf upgrade --refresh; read -rp \"Press Enter to close...\"'"
```

Fields:

| Key        | Description                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `command`  | Shell command run through `/bin/sh -lc`; each stdout line becomes a launcher result                                                                                                  |
| `exec`     | Optional shell command run when a result is activated; `{selection}` is the selected raw line, `{query}` is the typed text after the prefix                                          |
| `prefix`   | Optional launcher prefix such as `cmd` (without prefix symbol like `/`, which is configured by `provider_prefix`); if this is empty, set `global = true` or the entry is unreachable |
| `label`    | Provider title shown in the launcher provider overview; defaults to the entry id                                                                                                     |
| `glyph`    | Tabler glyph name for results; defaults to `terminal`                                                                                                                                |
| `global`   | Whether results participate in normal non-prefixed launcher search                                                                                                                   |
| `freeform` | Whether non-empty typed text can become an activatable result                                                                                                                        |

Noctalia runs `exec` as a detached command; it does not automatically open a terminal. For interactive commands such as
`dnf upgrade`, launch your terminal emulator explicitly (`foot -e ...`, `kitty -e ...`, `alacritty -e ...`, etc.).
That is different from Application results with `Terminal=true`, which use `$TERMINAL` / terminal discovery
(see [Shell settings](/noctalia/configuration/shell/#shell-settings)).

Set `freeform = true` when the typed text itself should be activatable. The typed text after the prefix is available as
`{query}`:

```toml
[shell.launcher.dmenu.entry.notify]
label = "Notify"
prefix = "notify"
glyph = "bell"
global = false
freeform = true
exec = "notify-send \"{query}\""
```

Typing `/notify Build finished` shows `Build finished` as a result. Activating it runs
`notify-send "Build finished"`. `command` is optional for freeform entries; add one only when you also want suggested
results.

## Text editing shortcuts

While the launcher input is focused, readline-style shortcuts are available:

| Shortcut            | Action                                  |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `Ctrl+A`            | Move caret to start of line             |
| `Ctrl+E`            | Move caret to end of line               |
| `Ctrl+B` / `Ctrl+F` | Move caret back / forward one character |
| `Alt+B`             | Move caret to start of previous word    |
| `Alt+F`             | Move caret to end of next word          |
| `Ctrl+W`            | Delete previous word                    |
| `Ctrl+U`            | Clear backward to start of line         |
| `Ctrl+K`            | Delete from caret to end of line        |
| `Alt+D`             | Delete next word                        |

## Configuration

The launcher itself is mostly automatic, but its behavior is influenced by other sections of your config:

- **[Keybinds](/noctalia/configuration/shell/#keybinds)**: Customize navigation (`up`, `down`) and activation (`validate`, `cancel`).
- **[Shell](/noctalia/configuration/shell/#shell-settings)**: `ui_scale` and `font_family` affect the launcher's appearance. `shell.launcher.categories` controls the category filters. `show_icons`, `show_app_origin_indicator`, `compact`, `app_grid`, `show_app_actions`, `sort_by_usage`, `pinned`, `fetch_exchange_rates`, and `auto_paste` adjust result layout, origin overlays, action search, ordering, pinning, currency rate refresh, and paste-after-copy (Settings → Launcher). `[shell.launcher.dmenu.entry.<id>]` adds custom dmenu-style providers backed by a shell command.
- **[Wallpaper](/noctalia/desktop/wallpaper/)**: Defines the search paths for the Wallpaper provider.
- **[Session actions](/noctalia/configuration/shell/#session-panel)**: Defines the actions shown by the Session provider.

### Provider prefixes

Every prefixed provider (built-in, dmenu, or plugin) is triggered the same way: a shared prefix character followed by a per-provider **trigger word**.

- `shell.launcher.provider_prefix` sets the common character (default `/`).
- Each provider supplies a bare trigger word, which is concatenated onto `provider_prefix`.

So `provider_prefix = "/"` with a trigger word of `emo` gives `/emo`. Changing `provider_prefix` to `.` makes every provider trigger on `.emo`, `.ssh`, and so on. An empty trigger word leaves a provider reachable only through the un-prefixed (global) search. Trigger words are always bare: write `ssh`, not `/ssh`.

Each provider type gets its trigger word from a different place:

- **Built-in providers** (Calculator, Clipboard, Date, Dictionary, Emoji, Files, Notes, Panels, Processes, Quicklinks, Screenshots, Scripts, Session, Snippets, System, Time, Timer, Wallpaper, WindowManagement, Windows): `[shell.launcher.providers.<name>]`.
- **Dmenu providers**: the `prefix`/`global` keys on the entry's `[shell.launcher.dmenu.entry.<id>]` table.
- **Plugin providers**: the `[[launcher_provider]]` manifest `prefix`, optionally overridden per-user via `[shell.launcher.providers."<pluginId>:<entry>"]`.

See [Shell: Launcher settings](/noctalia/configuration/shell/#shell-settings) for the full key reference, defaults, and examples.

### From the Settings UI

The built-in providers can be configured without editing your config, under **Settings → Panels → Launcher → Providers**:

- **Common Prefix Character**: the shared `provider_prefix` (defaults to `/`).
- **&lt;Provider&gt; Prefix**: the trigger word for each built-in provider. Leave blank to fall back to the built-in default.
- **&lt;Provider&gt; in Global Search**: toggles whether that provider also contributes results to the un-prefixed search.

Dmenu and plugin provider prefixes are configured only through their config tables (see the reference linked above); they do not appear in the Settings UI.
