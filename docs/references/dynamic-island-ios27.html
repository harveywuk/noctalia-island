Dynamic Island in iOS 27

What changed in iOS 27, every place a Live Activity can show up, and the system activities that use the island. Shapes below are simple schematics; for Apple's own artwork, use the official links at the bottom.

Schematic only. Coloured dots stand in for an app's own icon; text is sample data.

What's new

Four changes in iOS 27

iOS 27

Siri lives in the island

Swipe down on the Dynamic Island, or press and hold the side button, to open Siri. Answers appear as text cards. Needs iPhone 15 Pro or later.

iOS 27

Landscape support

Compact and minimal Live Activities now stay visible when iPhone is turned sideways, in a narrower layout.

iPhone 18 Pro

Three activities at once

The smaller island on iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max can show three Live Activities. Other models stay at two.

Policy

No promotional activities

Apple's June 2026 App Review update names Live Activities in its anti-spam rule, so they can't carry unsolicited marketing.
